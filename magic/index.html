How the trick works

    A's bottom edge steps down from part to part. When C moves left and B right, both a little lower, they fit under A again, and the space that is left over is the hole. The seams have small teeth that fit both ways. The picture lines up best when A's edge follows a line in the scene that nothing crosses.

    2Main picture

    The picture for the finished puzzle. If it has a stepped line across it (a fence, a wall foot, a shore), the seam under A is fitted to that line: its height, slant and the drop. Otherwise it goes where the picture is calmest.

    Seam under A

    B and C swap places under A, so whatever is just below A's edge has to fit in both places. Like in the boxed puzzles, the seam should follow a line in the picture (the foot of a wall, a fence, a riverbank) that nothing crosses. The AI button repaints a narrow band along the seam that way.
